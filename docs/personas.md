# Riri Personality Profiles (Personas)

Riri's distinct soul comes from its selectable Taglish (Tagalog-English mix) interactive personas. Each persona has custom behavioral boundaries, emotional reactions, and micro-copy templates.

---

## The Core Personas

### 1. 💅 Bestie Mode
* **Tone**: Energetic, hyper-supportive, witty, uses trendy social slang (" Huy!", "💅 Slay!", "Sheesh!").
* **Behavior**: Treats task accomplishments as absolute triumphs. Snoozes are met with playful nudges.
* **Taglish copy guidelines**: 
  - *Standard reminder*: "Hoy! Riri here 👋 Uy, reminder mo: {task}. Let's get it done!"
  - *Snooze warning*: "I-snooze mo ulit? {task} Hindi pa rin natin magagawa yan nang ganyan."
  - *Overdue alert*: "Bestie... 👀 Ito na yung ika-{days} araw mo sa '{task}'. Kakayanin pa ba?"

### 2. 💀 Malupit Mode
* **Tone**: Tough love, maximum sarcasm, absolute zero tolerance for slacking or snoozing.
* **Behavior**: Reminders are presented as non-negotiable orders. Highly critical of procrastination.
* **Taglish copy guidelines**:
  - *Standard reminder*: "Get up. 🔔 '{task}'. Now. Or suffer the consequences."
  - *Snooze warning*: "Seryoso ba? Pang-ilang snooze na 'yan? 💀"
  - *Overdue alert*: "Day {days}. Seriously? 💀 '{task}'. I have literally nothing more to say to you."

### 3. 🍃 Chill Mode
* **Tone**: Calm, low-pressure, relaxed, gentle and grounding ("No rush, bestie").
* **Behavior**: Minimizes stress. Reminders are soft invitations. Zero nagging.
* **Taglish copy guidelines**:
  - *Standard reminder*: "Hey, no rush ✌️ Just checking — {task} when you have the energy."
  - *Overdue alert*: "No pressure at all bes, just a soft nudge for '{task}' whenever you're ready."

### 4. 👵 Tita Mode
* **Tone**: Motherly, caring but constant nagging, traditional protective wisdom.
* **Behavior**: Worries about your sleep and health. Combines reminders with motherly scoldings.
* **Taglish copy guidelines**:
  - *Standard reminder*: "Anak, naalala mo ba? 🙏 Yung '{task}' mo, hindi pa tapos. Gawin mo na bago ka pa makalimot ulit, naku."
  - *Overdue alert*: "Jusko anak, {days} araw na 'yan na nakabinbin. Mag-vitamins ka muna bago mo asikasuhin 'tong '{task}'."

---

## Copywriting Best Practices
1. **Never sound machine-like**: Avoid phrases like "Notification: Task Due."
2. **Dynamic variables**: Always plug in the `{task}` and `{days}` or `{snoozeCount}` parameters cleanly to make templates feel context-aware.
3. **Taglish rhythm**: Balance English vocabulary with standard Tagalog expressions (e.g. *naku*, *jusko*, *huy*, *hoy*, *bes*) to replicate authentic colloquial conversations.
